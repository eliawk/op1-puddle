puddle

Browser mock-up of the screen, 320×160 scaled 3×, with a simulated input (one plucked note every 0.75 s) and the plugin's modulation laws. Same geometry, colours and laws as puddle_scene.c (0.9.2). The OP-1 draws it with its own renderer and font: thickness, glyphs and colours on the OLED will differ slightly.
